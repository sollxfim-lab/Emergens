How this connects

This page does not (and cannot) browse your server's filesystem — no page in a browser can. Instead, it expects modules/quick_menu.py to be running as a small local HTTP service at the address you enter above, exposing at minimum:

GET /status → 200 OK {"status":"online","service":"quick_menu","version":"1.0"}
  • Connect sends a real request to <scheme>://<ip:port>/status and waits up to 6 seconds for a reply.
  • Once connected, this page quietly re-checks every 15 seconds so the status stays accurate — if your quick_menu.py goes offline, it'll flip to "Connection Failed" on its own.
  • Your address is saved locally in this browser only, so it's ready the next time you open this page.
  • If your browser is loaded over https (a secure dashboard URL), most browsers will block plain http:// requests to a local server. Serve this dashboard over http, or give quick_menu.py a TLS certificate, if you hit a silent connection failure.
