Privacy Policy
Emergens

Emergens ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our web security testing platform, including the website, API, and related services (collectively, the "Service").

By accessing or using the Service, you agree to the practices described herein. If you do not agree, please discontinue use immediately.

01

Information We Collect

Personal Data

We may collect personally identifiable information that you voluntarily provide when you register, subscribe, or interact with the Service:

  • Account Information: name, email address, username, and hashed password.
  • Billing Information: payment details processed by third-party providers — we do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.
  • Communication Data: messages sent to our support team and their associated metadata.

Usage Data

Automatically collected when you interact with the Service:

  • IP address, browser type, operating system, and device identifiers.
  • Pages visited, features used, API call logs, and timestamps.
  • Scan targets and configuration, strictly for service functionality.

Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to maintain sessions, remember preferences, and analyze usage. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Cookies are optional

Essential cookies are required for authentication and security. Analytical cookies can be disabled without affecting core functionality.

02

How We Use Your Information

We use collected information for legitimate business purposes only:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service.
  • Process transactions and manage subscriptions.
  • Authenticate API requests and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Improve functionality through analytics and user feedback.
  • Send administrative emails, updates, and security alerts.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.
03

Disclosure of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: trusted third parties who assist in hosting, payment processing, email delivery, and analytics — bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: if required by law, subpoena, or valid governmental request.
  • Business Transfers: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or asset sale, with prior notice to you.
We never sell your data

Your personal information is never sold to advertisers, data brokers, or any third party for marketing purposes.

04

Data Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards including:

  • End-to-end encryption in transit using TLS 1.3.
  • Encryption at rest using AES-256 for sensitive data.
  • Role-based access controls and least-privilege policies.
  • Regular security audits and penetration testing.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Protect your API key

You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your API key and account credentials. Notify us immediately at security@emergens.io if you suspect unauthorized access.

05

Data Retention

We retain personal data as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. Scan results are retained for 30 days by default unless you request earlier deletion. You may request data deletion at any time by contacting us.

06

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  1. Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
  2. Object to or restrict processing of your information.
  3. Request data portability in a machine-readable format.
  4. Withdraw consent at any time without penalty.
  5. Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise these rights, email privacy@emergens.io. We will respond within 30 days.

07

International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, including Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) where applicable, to protect your data during cross-border transfers.

08

Children's Privacy

The Service is not intended for individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us immediately for removal.

09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date and, where appropriate, notified via email. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
