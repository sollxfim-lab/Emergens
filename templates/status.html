Status Page FAQ

How we measure uptime, what counts as an incident, and how you get notified.

Uptime is measured from three independent probe regions (Frankfurt, Singapore, Virginia) hitting health endpoints every 60 seconds. A minute counts as "up" only if at least two of three probes receive a valid HTTP 200 response within 3 seconds. Monthly uptime is the percentage of qualifying minutes.
A service is marked degraded when its error rate exceeds 1% over 5 minutes, or its P95 latency exceeds twice the trailing 7-day baseline, but the service is still responding to the majority of requests.
Incident notifications are dispatched within 60 seconds of an alert firing. Email subscribers receive the initial notice, every status update, and the final resolution summary. Telegram subscribers get the same feed instantly.
